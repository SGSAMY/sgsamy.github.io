Highlights

Impact across the data lifecycle

Data Platform & Engineering

  • Designed and delivered a cloud analytics warehouse on Azure Synapse, Snowflake, SQL Server and dbt, using layered Raw, Staging and Data Mart architecture with data quality, lineage and auditability built in.
  • Built automated ETL/ELT pipelines with Azure Data Factory and API integrations, consolidating data from multiple regulated source systems; automated reporting cut manual effort by 70% while improving data quality and consistency.

Business Intelligence & Reporting

  • Designed enterprise Power BI semantic models and dashboards with reusable DAX measures, time intelligence, row-level security and governed datasets, giving stakeholders trusted, high-performance reporting.
  • Integrated GA4, CRM and campaign data into a Single Customer View, enabling end-to-end customer journey analytics and better targeting across digital channels.

Customer & Advanced Analytics

  • Delivered insight-led campaign optimisation that contributed to an 8–10% uplift in conversion rates and a 12–15% increase in marketing ROI.
  • Evaluated marketing effectiveness with Marketing Mix Modelling and attribution, and built LTV, churn and predictive models in Python, informing budget allocation and retention strategy.

CRM & Campaign Management

  • Led multi-channel customer and campaign analytics across 100k+ customers a month, and delivered full-cycle email and SMS journeys for onboarding, renewals, re-engagement and C-SAT.
  • Delivered the Adobe Campaign Classic to Emarsys migration, including data mapping, validation and testing, with no break in CRM communications.

Digital & E-commerce

  • Delivered e-commerce and digital analytics across insurance and retail, from international e-commerce performance at Moss Bros to reporting for white-label insurance partners including Debenhams and Jet2.
  • Designed, built and launched the e-commerce platform for Sri Seafood, with online ordering and payments, KPI reporting, usability testing and paid Facebook and Instagram campaigns.

Leadership & Stakeholder Engagement

  • Partnered with Product, Marketing, Engineering and Operations stakeholders to translate complex business challenges into analytical requirements, KPIs and actionable recommendations supporting strategic and commercial decisions.
  • Managed and mentored analysts and developers through peer review, technical guidance and 1-2-1s, set Power BI and data quality standards, and ran delivery through Azure DevOps with Git and CI/CD.
