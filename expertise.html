Key skills

What I bring to a data team

Data Engineering & ETL/ELT

Designing and delivering scalable data pipelines using SQL Server, Azure Data Factory, Microsoft Fabric, Python and dbt, including API integrations that consolidate customer and operational data from multiple regulated source systems into reliable, analytics-ready datasets.

Data Modelling & Warehousing

Kimball dimensional modelling with fact and dimension tables and star schemas, and governed warehouses built in layered Raw, Staging and Data Mart architectures that give BI and analytics a trusted foundation.

Power BI & Data Visualisation

Power BI Desktop and Service, advanced DAX, Power Query (M) and semantic models, plus row-level security, gateways, scheduled refresh and performance optimisation. Also Tableau and SSRS.

Customer & Marketing Analytics

Segmentation, customer lifecycle and journey analytics, LTV/CLV, churn and conversion analysis, cohort analysis, A/B testing, attribution and Marketing Mix Modelling to improve acquisition, retention and marketing effectiveness.

Predictive & Advanced Analytics

Predictive and statistical models in Python and scikit-learn for churn prediction, campaign conversion, demand forecasting and performance measurement.

Digital & E-commerce Analytics

GA4, digital customer journeys, website behaviour, conversion and online sales analysis, alongside building e-commerce platforms, analytics tracking and KPI frameworks.

Campaign & CRM Data Solutions

Full-cycle email and SMS lifecycle campaigns across Adobe Campaign Classic, Emarsys, Ometria and Pure360 (now Spotler), including segmentation, automation, measurement and CRM platform migration.

Data Governance & Delivery

Data quality, validation, lineage and auditability within regulated environments (GDPR, FCA, AML), with controlled delivery through Azure DevOps, Git, peer-reviewed pull requests and CI/CD.
